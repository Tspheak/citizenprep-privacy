Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

CitizenPrep is an independent study app made by Tun Labs. It helps you practice the civics questions for the U.S. naturalization interview. This policy says what the app does with information.

CitizenPrep is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, or any government agency. It does not give legal advice. The questions can contain errors. Confirm anything important with the official USCIS study materials: https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/find-study-materials-and-resources/study-for-the-test

No account, and no data on our servers

You do not create an account. Tun Labs does not run a server that collects your name, email, quiz scores, or other personal information. The app does not ask you to sign in.

What stays on your device

The app saves some information only on your phone, using the device's local storage (shared preferences). That information is not uploaded to the developer. It includes:

  • Quiz history, including scores and the answers you chose
  • Settings, such as Audio Study options and whether study reminders are turned off
  • Whether Premium is unlocked on this device
  • The filing-date choice you pick so the app can show the 2008 or 2025 civics test
  • A local copy of the civics questions, so study can work offline

You can clear quiz history inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes the local data.

Civics questions and Firebase

The questions ship inside the app. The app can also download updated questions from Google Firebase Realtime Database. It only reads public question content. It does not send your quiz answers, scores, or filing-date choice to Firebase.

When the app contacts Firebase, Google may process technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the request. Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising

The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Premium turns those ads off. The app does not show interstitial ads.

Google AdMob may collect device or other identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions/usage data, and diagnostics (crash and performance data). This data is shared with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. AdMob may show personalized ads based on your device's ad settings. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Premium users are not shown ads. Ads are also not shown on platforms where ads are not supported.

Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Premium is optional. On Android it is sold by Google Play Billing. The products are:

  • premium_monthly, a subscription that renews until you cancel it
  • premium_lifetime, a one-time purchase

Prices are set by Google Play, not inside this policy. The app does not start a free trial on its own.

Google Play processes the payment. Tun Labs never receives your card number, billing address, or other payment details. The app only stores a yes-or-no Premium flag on the device. You can restore a purchase from the Premium screen.

To cancel the monthly subscription: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile photo, tap Payments & subscriptions, tap Subscriptions, tap CitizenPrep, then tap Cancel. Canceling stops future renewals. It does not erase the time you already paid for. The lifetime purchase is not a subscription. Refunds follow Google Play's rules.

This version of the app checks purchases with Google Play. It does not include a separate App Store purchase setup.

Text-to-speech

Audio Study speaks with your phone's built-in voice (the flutter_tts plugin). Speech stays on the device. The app does not send the spoken text to the developer.

Notifications

The app can schedule local study reminders on your device. Those reminders are not sent through a developer server. If you turn reminders off, that choice is saved on the device. You can also turn notifications off in the device settings. The app may ask for notification permission so the reminders can appear.

Children

CitizenPrep is not directed at children under 13. It is a study tool for the naturalization interview. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top changes when we do. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy. Check back periodically.
